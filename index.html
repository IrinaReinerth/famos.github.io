beyond planning

Your system shouldn’t just organize your life. It should help you improve it.

FAMOS helps you notice friction, understand what is already working and redesign the parts of family life that still feel heavier than they need to.

a real-life problem
Laundry · 7/10
I wash every day and baskets where we undress work well, but towels and miscellaneous laundry still end up in piles by the washing machine. How can I improve the flow?
FAMOS
Keep the collection baskets where clothes naturally come off. The friction is at the washing-machine area: give the loose categories 2–3 clearly defined homes so you stop repeatedly deciding what each pile is and what to wash next.

FAMOS doesn’t assume everything needs fixing. It preserves what works and focuses on where decisions, clutter or repeated effort accumulate.

Before you start

Do I need a paid version of ChatGPT?

No. For the Starter experience, use a ChatGPT plan that supports the conversation and file features you need.

How long does setup take?

Usually 20–40 minutes, depending on your household and how detailed you want the system to be.

What if my system feels too complicated?

Ask FAMOS to simplify it to one Today view and no more than three key focus tasks.
